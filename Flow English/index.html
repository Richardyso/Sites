Conteúdo Online Book 1 (To Be)

1. THE PRESENT TENSE (Presente)

Meaning: Eu sou / Eu estou.

A. Affirmative Form (+)

Example: HAPPY (Feliz)

  • 1. I am happy.
  • 2. You are happy.
  • 3. He is happy.
  • 4. She is happy.
  • 5. It is happy.
  • 6. We are happy.
  • 7. You are happy. (Plural)
  • 8. They are happy.

B. Negative Form (-)

Example: TIRED (Cansado)

  • 1. I am not tired. (I'm not)
  • 2. You are not tired. (aren't)
  • 3. He is not tired. (isn't)
  • 4. She is not tired. (isn't)
  • 5. It is not tired. (isn't)
  • 6. We are not tired. (aren't)
  • 7. You are not tired. (aren't)
  • 8. They are not tired. (aren't)

C. Interrogative Form (?)

O verbo vem antes do sujeito. Example: READY (Pronto)

  • 1. Am I ready?
  • 2. Are you ready?
  • 3. Is he ready?
  • 4. Is she ready?
  • 5. Is it ready?
  • 6. Are we ready?
  • 7. Are you ready?
  • 8. Are they ready?

2. THE PAST TENSE (Passado)

Meaning: Eu era / Eu estava.

Atenção: Singular = WAS | Plural = WERE

A. Affirmative Form (+)

Example: AT HOME (Em casa)

  • 1. I was at home.
  • 2. You were at home.
  • 3. He was at home.
  • 4. She was at home.
  • 5. It was at home.
  • 6. We were at home.
  • 7. You were at home.
  • 8. They were at home.

B. Negative Form (-)

Example: SICK (Doente)

  • 1. I was not sick. (wasn't)
  • 2. You were not sick. (weren't)
  • 3. He was not sick. (wasn't)
  • 4. She was not sick. (wasn't)
  • 5. It was not sick. (wasn't)
  • 6. We were not sick. (weren't)
  • 7. You were not sick. (weren't)
  • 8. They were not sick. (weren't)

C. Interrogative Form (?)

Example: LATE (Atrasado)

  • 1. Was I late?
  • 2. Were you late?
  • 3. Was he late?
  • 4. Was she late?
  • 5. Was it late?
  • 6. Were we late?
  • 7. Were you late?
  • 8. Were they late?

3. THE FUTURE TENSE (Futuro)

Meaning: Eu serei / Eu estarei.

Todos usam "Will be".

A. Affirmative Form (+)

Example: HERE (Aqui)

  • 1. I will be here.
  • 2. You will be here.
  • 3. He will be here.
  • 4. She will be here.
  • 5. It will be here.
  • 6. We will be here.
  • 7. You will be here.
  • 8. They will be here.

B. Negative Form (-)

Todos usam "Won't be". Example: ANGRY (Com raiva)

  • 1. I won't be angry.
  • 2. You won't be angry.
  • 3. He won't be angry.
  • 4. She won't be angry.
  • 5. It won't be angry.
  • 6. We won't be angry.
  • 7. You won't be angry.
  • 8. They won't be angry.

C. Interrogative Form (?)

Example: FAMOUS (Famoso)

  • 1. Will I be famous?
  • 2. Will you be famous?
  • 3. Will he be famous?
  • 4. Will she be famous?
  • 5. Will it be famous?
  • 6. Will we be famous?
  • 7. Will you be famous?
  • 8. Will they be famous?

Dialogue 1: Conversation Practice

Instructions: Pair up. Student A is John. Student B is Mary. Read, then swap roles.

John: Hello. My name is John.

Mary: Hello John. I am Mary. I am an English student. What are you?

John: I am an engineer. Were you in class yesterday?

Mary: No, I wasn't. I was at home.

John: Will you be here tomorrow?

Mary: Yes, I will be.

Conteúdo Online Book 2 (Action Verbs)

1. PRESENT TENSE (Rotina / Hábito)

Verbo modelo: TO WORK (Trabalhar)

Regra: He/She/It ganha "S" na afirmação e usa "Does" na pergunta/negação.

A. Affirmative Form (+)

  • 1. I work every day.
  • 2. You work every day.
  • 3. He works every day. (Atenção: S)
  • 4. She works every day. (Atenção: S)
  • 5. It works every day. (Atenção: S)
  • 6. We work every day.
  • 7. You work every day.
  • 8. They work every day.

B. Negative Form (-)

Auxiliar: Don't / Doesn't

  • 1. I don't work on Sundays.
  • 2. You don't work on Sundays.
  • 3. He doesn't work on Sundays. (Atenção: DOESN'T)
  • 4. She doesn't work on Sundays.
  • 5. It doesn't work on Sundays.
  • 6. We doesn't work on Sundays.
  • 7. You don't work on Sundays.
  • 8. They don't work on Sundays.

C. Interrogative Form (?)

Auxiliar: Do / Does (O verbo perde o S)

  • 1. Do I work here?
  • 2. Do you work here?
  • 3. Does he work here? (Atenção: DOES)
  • 4. Does she work here?
  • 5. Does it work here?
  • 6. Do we work here?
  • 7. Do you work here?
  • 8. Do they work here?

2. PAST TENSE (Ação Finalizada)

Verbo modelo: TO WORK (Trabalhar)

Regra: Todos usam "ED" na afirmação e "DID" na pergunta/negação. (Verbos Regulares)

A. Affirmative Form (+)

  • 1. I worked yesterday.
  • 2. You worked yesterday.
  • 3. He worked yesterday.
  • 4. She worked yesterday.
  • 5. It worked yesterday.
  • 6. We worked yesterday.
  • 7. You worked yesterday.
  • 8. They worked yesterday.

B. Negative Form (-)

Auxiliar: DIDN'T (O verbo volta ao normal: Work)

  • 1. I didn't work last night.
  • 2. You didn't work last night.
  • 3. He didn't work last night.
  • 4. She didn't work last night.
  • 5. It didn't work last night.
  • 6. We didn't work last night.
  • 7. You didn't work last night.
  • 8. They didn't work last night.

C. Interrogative Form (?)

Auxiliar: DID (O verbo volta ao normal: Work)

  • 1. Did I work well?
  • 2. Did you work well?
  • 3. Did he work well?
  • 4. Did she work well?
  • 5. Did it work well?
  • 6. Did we work well?
  • 7. Did you work well?
  • 8. Did they work well?

3. FUTURE TENSE (Planos)

Verbo modelo: TO WORK (Trabalhar)

Regra: O mais fácil de todos. Use WILL para todo mundo.

A. Affirmative Form (+)

  • 1. I will work tomorrow.
  • 2. You will work tomorrow.
  • 3. He will work tomorrow.
  • 4. She will work tomorrow.
  • 5. It will work tomorrow.
  • 6. We will work tomorrow.
  • 7. You will work tomorrow.
  • 8. They will work tomorrow.

B. Negative Form (-)

Auxiliar: WON'T

  • 1. I won't work alone.
  • 2. You won't work alone.
  • 3. He won't work alone.
  • 4. She won't work alone.
  • 5. It won't work alone.
  • 6. We won't work alone.
  • 7. You won't work alone.
  • 8. They won't work alone.

C. Interrogative Form (?)

Auxiliar: WILL (Pula para frente)

  • 1. Will I work with you?
  • 2. Will you work with me?
  • 3. Will he work with us?
  • 4. Will she work with us?
  • 5. Will it work well?
  • 6. Will we work together?
  • 7. Will you work together?
  • 8. Will they work together?

Dialogue 2: The Interview

Instructions: Pair up. Student A is the Boss. Student B is the Candidate.

Boss: Good morning. Do you work currently?

Candidate: No, I don't work right now. I left my job last month.

Boss: I see. Where did you work before?

Candidate: I worked at a bank for 5 years.

Boss: Did you like the job?

Candidate: Yes, I liked it, but I wanted a change.

Boss: Okay. If I hire you, will you work on weekends?

Candidate: Yes, I will. I will work hard for this company.
